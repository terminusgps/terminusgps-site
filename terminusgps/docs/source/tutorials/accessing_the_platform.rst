Accessing the platform
======================

The Terminus GPS platform is accessible at this URL:

https://hosting.terminusgps.com/

==========
Logging in
==========

After navigating to the `Terminus GPS platform`_, you'll be presented with the login form.

Enter your Terminus GPS username into the ``User`` box.

Enter your Terminus GPS password into the ``Password`` box.
